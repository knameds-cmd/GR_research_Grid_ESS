## 1. Research question
Which battery business models are profitable in the UK electricity market, and how effective are policy mechanisms supporting battery storage? (from abstract)

## 2. Setting & assumptions
- Value streams: arbitrage in electricity markets, Capacity Market auctions, ancillary services (frequency response, reserve) (from abstract).
- Battery technologies compared include advanced lead-acid and lithium-ion (from abstract). Data years, price-taker and foresight assumptions: not verified (likely pre-2020 data, published Jan 2021).

## 7. Key results (from abstract)
- Lowest payback periods for premium ancillary services: **3.3 years** (advanced lead-acid providing Fast Reserve) and **6.6 years** (Li-ion providing frequency response).
- Promising returns from stacking STOR with balancing-market arbitrage.

## 8. Limitations (critical reading)
- Pre-DC (2020) and pre-EAC (2023) product set; Fast Reserve and STOR have since been replaced (QR 2024, SR 2026). Useful as the "before" baseline of the UK business-model literature, not for current numbers.
