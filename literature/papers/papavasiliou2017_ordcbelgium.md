## 1. Research question
Can an energy-only design in which real-time energy prices carry operating-reserve-demand-curve (ORDC) scarcity adders remunerate flexible capacity in Belgium enough for CCGTs to recover investment costs? (from abstract)

## 2. Setting & assumptions
Belgian market model over 21 months; comparison of energy prices with and without ORDC adders reflecting the scarcity value of reserve capacity. (from abstract) Model specifics (reserve dimensioning, loss-of-load probability function, VOLL) not read.

## 3. Constraints that drove the model choice
Need to estimate the "missing money" for flexible units from scarcity value that existing European energy-only markets do not price. (from abstract, interpretation)

## 4. Model
Simulation of Belgian real-time operations; ORDC adder = f(available reserve capacity) added to real-time energy price (per Hogan's ORDC concept). Not read in detail.

## 5. Data & processing
21 months of Belgian system data (period not read).

## 6. Justification
Counterfactual comparison of the same historical/simulated operation with vs without scarcity adders; investment-cost recovery as metric. (from abstract)

## 7. Key results (from abstract)
- Without adders, existing CCGTs in Belgium cannot recover investment costs.
- ORDC adders reflecting the true scarcity value of capacity restore economic viability for most CCGTs.

## 8. Limitations (stated + critical reading)
Generator-centric (no explicit storage); remuneration effect depends on ORDC parameters (VOLL, LOLP) chosen by the regulator. Full text not read.

## 9. Relevance to my study
Scarcity pricing is a market-rule lever with large implications for battery revenue (batteries capture short scarcity spikes). For a Belgian-data simulator, an ORDC-adder toggle is a natural counterfactual rule; Belgium's later adoption of a scarcity-pricing-related mechanism in balancing (and the capacity remuneration mechanism) makes this the reference evaluation. Methodological pattern: ex-post counterfactual price reconstruction on historical data.
